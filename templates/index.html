ABOUT THE RESEARCH ASSISTANT

How PolicySage works

Research public Indian insurance information and inspect the evidence behind an answer.

  1. Ask a question.Ask about policy terms, claims, exclusions or waiting periods. Follow-up questions use the current conversation.
  2. Find supporting information.PolicySage searches its indexed documents, checks available evidence and may consult official websites if permitted and needed.
  3. Review the answer.Inspect citations, source excerpts, available document links and the completed research steps. Missing evidence should be stated clearly.

Informational research only — not confirmation of personal coverage or a claims decision. New chat and page refresh clear the conversation.
